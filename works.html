Catalogue · Acrylic on canvas

Works

This body of work explores music as a living force, embedded in human existence. In it, I investigate how rhythm shapes identity, how performance reveals vulnerability, and how music functions as cultural memory and therapy.

It contributes to contemporary African art discourse by positioning music not only as a subject matter but also as a conceptual framework, highlighting performance as vulnerability and sound as a cultural continuity.

— John Akande
