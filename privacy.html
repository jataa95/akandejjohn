Privacy Policy

How this website handles information about you. In short: it has no accounts, sets no cookies and does not sell or share your details.

Who we are

This website, johnakande.studio, is the portfolio of John Tobi Akande, a figurative artist based in Lagos, Nigeria. In this policy, “we” and “John” mean him.

For any question about your information, email johnakande23@gmail.com.

What we collect

You can browse the whole site without giving us any personal information. The only personal information John receives is what you choose to send him:

  • Enquiries and print requests. The contact form and the “Request a print” button do not send anything through this website. They open your own email app with a message already written; nothing is sent until you press send there. John then receives your email, which includes your email address, your name if you give it, and your message.
  • Payments. If you buy a painting, John receives the bank transfer details your bank shares with him and any receipt you send.

How we use it

John uses your messages only to reply to you, to arrange a sale, commission, print, exhibition or live painting, and to keep records of sales. He does not sell, rent or trade your information, and he does not add you to a mailing list without asking.

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not set cookies, so there is no cookie banner. It saves three small preferences in your own browser, which never leave your device:

  • the currency you chose for prices on the Works page;
  • the day’s exchange rates, so they are not downloaded again for 12 hours;
  • a note that the opening animation has already played during this visit.

You can clear these at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

Services that help run the site

Like any website, loading these pages means your browser connects to the services below. They receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type, under their own privacy policies:

  • Vercel, which hosts the website and keeps standard server logs. Vercel Web Analytics may also count page visits; it does not use cookies and reports only totals, not individual visitors.
  • Google Fonts and Fontshare, which provide the typefaces.
  • open.er-api.com, which provides exchange rates when prices are shown in another currency.
  • Instagram and Threads, only if you follow a link to them.

How long we keep it

Emails are kept for as long as they are useful for replying to you and for John’s sales and tax records, and are then deleted.

Your choices

You can ask John to see the information he holds about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing johnakande23@gmail.com. He will reply as soon as he can.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
