The process

How it works

For reference, catalogue works range from 28 × 24 in at $1,000 to 7 × 5 ft at $12,000.

  1. 01

    Share your idea

    Tell John what you have in mind: the subject, the space it’s for, and the size you imagine.

  2. 02

    Agree the details

    Size, price and timeline are agreed together before any paint touches the canvas.

  3. 03

    In the studio

    John paints the work by hand in his Lagos studio, in acrylic on canvas.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Collection or shipping of the finished painting is arranged with you.
