Selected Works

Figurative paintings of young musicians, warm interiors and the instruments that open a world — made in Lagos.

John Akande is a Nigerian figurative artist based in Lagos whose work captures the essence of African culture through the relationship between visual art and sound.

Largely self-taught before expanding his creative perspective at the Yaba College of Technology, his practice merges expressionism and realism to explore how music shapes identity, preserves heritage, and functions as a universal language.

Working primarily with acrylic on canvas, Akande uses bold contrasts, textured layers and vibrant palettes to translate the physical movement and emotional intensity of musicians into visual form.
