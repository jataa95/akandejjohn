About the artist

John Akande is a contemporary Nigerian figurative artist whose practice explores human expression, cultural identity, music and the complexities of everyday life.

Formally trained at Yaba College of Technology, Lagos, he works primarily in acrylic on canvas, alongside mixed media, combining realism and expressionism to build emotionally engaging visual narratives.

Music holds a significant place in his practice. His works have been exhibited in Nigeria and internationally, in group exhibitions and online presentations.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Can’t find what you need? Ask John directly.

01How do I buy a painting?

Open any painting in the catalogue and press “Enquire about this work”, or use the contact form. John replies personally to confirm availability, the final price and the next steps.

02What currency are the prices in?

Prices are set in US dollars. The currency switch on the Works page shows approximate amounts in euros, pounds and naira, converted at the day’s rate; the final price is agreed in US dollars.

03Can I commission a painting?

Yes. Share your idea, the space it’s for and the size you imagine, and the size, price and timeline are agreed together before work begins. See how commissions work.

04How is a painting delivered?

Collection or delivery is arranged with each collector. Tell John where you’re based when you enquire, and he’ll confirm the options and costs.

05What are the paintings made with, and how big are they?

John works primarily in acrylic on canvas. Catalogue works range from 28 × 24 in to 7 × 5 ft; each painting’s page lists its size in inches and centimetres.

06How do I pay for a painting?

By bank transfer to John’s Access Bank accounts: the naira account for payments in naira, or the domiciliary account (with its SWIFT code) for international payments. Please confirm availability and the final price with John before paying. See payment details.

07Does John paint live at events?

Yes. Live painting turns the music and movement of an event into a finished canvas. Choose “Live painting” on the contact form to ask about a date.
