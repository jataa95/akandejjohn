About the artist

John Akande is a contemporary Nigerian figurative artist whose practice explores human expression, cultural identity, music and the complexities of everyday life.

Formally trained at Yaba College of Technology, Lagos, he works primarily in acrylic on canvas, alongside mixed media, combining realism and expressionism to build emotionally engaging visual narratives.

Music holds a significant place in his practice. His works have been exhibited in Nigeria and internationally, including Malta, in group exhibitions and online presentations.
